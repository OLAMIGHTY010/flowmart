# FlowMart Super App - Product Requirement Document (PRD)

## 1. Executive Summary
**Vision:** "One App. Everything You Need."
FlowMart is Nigeria’s first true Everyday Super App. It consolidates e-commerce, food delivery, grocery shopping, logistics, service booking, and digital payments into a single, unified, hyper-local platform. Instead of managing 10 different apps, users rely on FlowMart as their daily operating system for commerce.

## 2. Problem Statement
The Nigerian digital commerce landscape is heavily fragmented:
- **Scattered Utilities:** Users jump between Jumia (shopping), Jiji (classifieds), Chowdeck/Glovo (food), WhatsApp/IG (local vendors), and banking apps.
- **Friction & Redundancy:** Multiple wallets, separate KYC, repetitive onboarding, and fragmented customer support.
- **Trust Deficit:** High fraud rates on social commerce and classifieds.
- **Logistics Bottlenecks:** Slow delivery times due to non-localized dispatch systems.

## 3. The FlowMart Solution
FlowMart combines everything into one seamless ecosystem:
- **Hyperlocal Commerce Engine:** Automatically surfaces businesses, artisans, and products closest to the user to minimize delivery time and cost.
- **Unified Identity & Wallet:** One login and one digital wallet powering all services.
- **AI Shopping Assistant:** NLP-driven search and recommendations (e.g., "I need ingredients for jollof rice").
- **Trust First:** Rigorous merchant and rider verification (BVN, NIN, CAC), Escrow payments, and Seller Ratings.

## 4. Target Personas
- **The Convenience Seeker (B2C):** Busy professionals looking for quick food, groceries, and reliable services without juggling apps.
- **The Local Merchant (B2B):** Small business owners, Instagram vendors, and restaurants needing a storefront, inventory management, and reliable logistics.
- **The Service Provider (Gig Worker):** Artisans and delivery riders seeking steady income streams within their local vicinity.

## 5. Core Modules & Features

### 5.1. Customer App (The Super App interface)
- **Unified Search:** Searches across food, products, and services.
- **Digital Wallet:** Fund, transfer, pay bills, and use Escrow.
- **Verticals:** Food, Groceries, Pharmacy, Marketplace (New & Used), Services, Real Estate, Vehicles.
- **AI Assistant:** Chat interface for complex queries and cart building.
- **Community Commerce:** Wishlists, referral systems, and merchant subscriptions.

### 5.2. Merchant App (Business Dashboard)
- **Storefront Management:** Inventory, promotions, and order fulfillment.
- **Financial Dashboard:** Earnings, AI pricing suggestions, and payouts.
- **Marketing Hub:** Ad management and customer analytics.

### 5.3. Rider / Logistics App
- **Geo-Optimized Dispatch:** Auto-assigns orders based on hyper-local proximity.
- **Earning Tracker:** Real-time wallet and performance metrics.
- **Safety & Verification:** In-app emergency protocols and identity verification.

## 6. Success Metrics (KPIs)
- **DAU/MAU Ratio:** High daily engagement driven by food, groceries, and payments.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Growth over time as trust increases.
- **Cross-Vertical Usage:** Percentage of users who use 3+ different services on the platform monthly.
- **Delivery Time:** Average fulfillment time across food (target: <45m) and groceries (target: <20m).

## 7. Assumptions & Constraints
- Assumes stable internet penetration and smartphone adoption in target demographics.
- Must comply strictly with CBN regulations regarding digital wallets and escrow.
- Hyperlocal matching relies heavily on precise geolocation mapping (Google Maps API integration).
